# Episode 001 — Wi-Fi Ka Pressure Cooker

## Core idea

Riya proudly claims she has invented a pressure cooker that gives free Wi-Fi. Her family believes her because the cooker really makes a strange “Wi-Fi connected” sound. The final reveal: she has secretly connected the cooker whistle to the watchman's old Wi-Fi router, and every whistle resets the password.

## Production plan

- **Language:** Natural Hindi with short lines
- **Style:** Expressive polished 3D animation
- **Format:** Vertical 9:16
- **Length:** 30 seconds, six clips of five seconds
- **Setting:** Small Indian apartment kitchen opening into the society corridor
- **Cast:** Riya, Meera, Guard Kadam
- **Props:** Pressure cooker, smartphone, small Wi-Fi router, steel plate
- **Comedy safety:** No fire, electric shock, or dangerous handling. Show the cooker safely switched off before Riya touches it.

## Character continuity

Riya keeps her saved appearance: shoulder-length wavy black hair half tied back, electric-magenta sparkly cropped jacket over a black top, black flared trousers, silver hoop earrings, and white sneakers. Meera keeps her mustard sari with turquoise border and long braid. Guard Kadam keeps his navy watchman uniform, cap, and black moustache. No extras.

## Six-shot script

### Shot 1 — Hook: 0–5 seconds

Visual: Riya bursts into the kitchen holding a smartphone above a pressure cooker like a scientist. The phone shows a Wi-Fi icon with one bar. Meera looks doubtful.

Riya: “Meera, maine pressure cooker ko Wi-Fi bana diya!”

Audio: Fast comic sting, cooker hiss, phone connection beep.

### Shot 2 — False proof: 5–10 seconds

Visual: Riya taps the cooker lid. The cooker gives one loud whistle. The phone suddenly shows full Wi-Fi bars. Meera’s eyes become huge.

Meera: “Arre! Sach mein signal aa gaya!”

Audio: Loud whistle followed by a bright connection sound.

### Shot 3 — Escalation: 10–15 seconds

Visual: Riya places a steel plate beside the cooker and poses proudly. She tells Meera to call Guard Kadam because the whole society can use the new Wi-Fi. Meera runs toward the corridor.

Riya: “Ab poori building online chalegi!”

Audio: Heroic mini music cue and quick running footsteps.

### Shot 4 — False success: 15–20 seconds

Visual: Guard Kadam arrives with his phone. Riya carefully points at the cooker. Another whistle makes all three phones show full signal. Kadam smiles and starts a video call.

Kadam: “Wah, watchman ka network upgrade!”

Audio: Cooker whistle, phone notification burst, happy reaction.

### Shot 5 — Reversal: 20–25 seconds

Visual: The old Wi-Fi router on Kadam’s desk starts blinking wildly in the corridor. Each cooker whistle changes the router password. A small paper beside it shows: “Password: whistle number.” Kadam realises what happened.

Kadam: “Riya, tumne Wi-Fi nahi… mera password paka diya!”

Audio: Record scratch, rapid router beeps, Riya’s nervous gulp.

### Shot 6 — Final payoff: 25–30 seconds

Visual: Riya tries one final whistle. Every phone disconnects. Kadam chases her around the corridor holding the router; Meera laughs. Riya hides behind the steel plate.

Riya: “Free Wi-Fi tha… password paidal chala gaya!”

End on Riya’s guilty smile and Kadam’s exaggerated shocked face.
