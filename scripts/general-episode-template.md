# General Hindi Comedy Episode Script Template

Use this template for every new Jokes Apart episode. Replace the bracketed text with a fresh original idea.

## Episode setup

- **Title:** [short funny title]
- **Premise:** [one sentence describing the everyday problem]
- **Setting:** [one simple Indian location]
- **Language:** Natural Hindi or Marathi, one main language per episode
- **Style:** Expressive 3D animation, vertical 9:16
- **Target length:** 25–35 seconds, usually 6 clips of 4–5 seconds
- **Main cast:** Riya plus one or two recurring characters

## Fixed character rule

Use the saved Riya reference in every episode. Keep her face, wavy shoulder-length hair, age, body shape, electric-magenta sparkly jacket, black top, black flared trousers, hoop earrings, and white sneakers unchanged. Add only the characters required by the joke. No random people or wardrobe changes.

## Six-beat story

### Beat 1 — Immediate hook, 4–5 seconds

Show the problem in the first frame. Riya is already doing something surprising or overconfident. Use a strong visual action and one short line if needed.

**Prompt:** [Riya action] in [setting]. Start with movement. Make the problem clear without narration. Exactly [number] named adults, no extras.

### Beat 2 — Confident plan, 4–5 seconds

Riya explains or demonstrates her plan. The plan should look believable for one moment but slightly too confident.

**Dialogue:** “ [short natural Hindi or Marathi line] ”

### Beat 3 — Escalation, 4–5 seconds

The plan becomes bigger, faster, or more awkward. Add one visible prop and a strong reaction from another character.

**Action:** [specific physical escalation]

### Beat 4 — False success, 4–5 seconds

The characters think the plan worked. Hold the confidence for a short moment so the reversal feels stronger.

**Audio:** [specific sound effect, music cue, or short dialogue]

### Beat 5 — Visual reversal, 4–5 seconds

Reveal the unexpected consequence. Make the reversal visible even with the sound muted. Avoid explanations.

**Action:** [safe, harmless consequence]

### Beat 6 — Final reaction, 4–5 seconds

End on the funniest face, dance, pose, or one-line reaction. Cut quickly after the laugh.

**Final line:** “ [short punchline] ”

## Production rules

- Use vertical 9:16 for image generation, video generation, timeline, and export.
- Use Creative mode and Advanced mode when available.
- Use Manual Video Length consistently; do not exceed the planned total runtime.
- Use one simple location and a small number of props.
- Every clip needs movement, a facial reaction, or a clear prop action.
- Keep sound effects loud enough to understand, with dialogue above the music.
- Do not use copied footage, copied dialogue, copyrighted song lyrics, dangerous stunts, cruelty, explosions, or unsafe electrical activity.
- Generate, save, and reuse the best character reference image before creating the clips.
- Assemble all clips in story order, auto-align the timeline, save the project, and verify the final MP4 before reporting completion.

## Quality check

Before export, confirm:

1. The joke is understandable from the first shot.
2. The reversal is different from the setup.
3. The final reaction is visible and funny.
4. Riya looks consistent in every shot.
5. No unwanted person, object, text, or logo appears.
6. The final video is portrait and contains all planned clips.
